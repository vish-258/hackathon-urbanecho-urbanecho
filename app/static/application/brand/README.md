# UrbanEcho visual identity

The wordmark and waveform icon are lossless crops from the user-supplied `Urban-Echo-Concept.mp4` at 00:03 (1920 × 1080 source). The film remains outside the repository. The wordmark is 224 × 52 px and the mark is 52 × 52 px; retain their proportions and avoid enlarging them beyond their source dimensions. Their opaque background matches the navigation frame, `--chrome: #0d1b21`.

On 10 October 2026, the product owner requested a warm, approachable modern-city personality with a balance of light and dark. The resulting **Limestone & Terracotta** palette pairs the original dark navigation frame with limestone work areas (`#f1eee6`), ivory panels (`#fffcf6`), and clay actions (`#a14432`). Green (`#356647`), amber (`#765400`), and coral (`#963e34`) retain distinct status roles with text labels. Dark navigation uses lighter counterparts for contrast; chart series use darker, differentiated colors and existing dash patterns on light surfaces.

The name, original assets, thin dividers, actual readings, units, calibration labels and workflows remain in place; concept-film example data is never imported. Fonts use Arial/Helvetica/system sans-serif without a third-party font service. Earlier theme verification documents describe their dated dark-palette versions, rather than the current palette.

Navigation and summary icons are Tabler Icons v3.35.0, MIT-licensed, from https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons. Their license is in `icons/LICENSE`. They are served locally; there are no new runtime requests to an icon provider.
